When you share something on GainForest.app, it helps to know where it goes and who can see it. This page tries to explain that simply. It is a short guide, not a legal document: the [privacy policy](https://www.gainforest.app/privacy) has the full details, and GainForest.app keeps its own, longer page at [Your account and your data](https://www.gainforest.app/en/your-data).

## Your account is not held by GainForest

You sign in to GainForest.app with a **Certified** account. Certified is run by the Hypercerts Foundation, a nonprofit, and it stores your posts in a space that belongs to you.

GainForest.app is one app that reads from and writes to that space. Other apps on the same open network (the AT Protocol, which Bluesky also uses) can do the same, so your work is not locked inside GainForest. GainForest cannot see your password or sign in as you.

## Most of what you post is public

Your profile, sightings, photos, sound recordings, sites, Projects, and Bumicerts can be seen by anyone, including other apps. Only what you use to sign in, such as your email address and sign-in codes, stays private.

<PublicPrivate />

<Callout type="warning">
Please do not post the exact location of rare species or protected places, or people who have not agreed to be shown.
</Callout>

Because posts are public, other apps or websites may keep copies. If you delete something later, GainForest cannot remove those copies.

## How your data is used

GainForest is a nonprofit. Your data is used to run the app: to show your work on maps, profiles, and Projects, to connect it to organizations, BioBlitz rounds, and funding, and to keep the service safe. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

<DataUse />

## Removing your data

You can delete a single post yourself at [gainforest.app](https://www.gainforest.app). To delete your whole account, email the Certified team at [support@hypercerts.org](mailto:support@hypercerts.org) from the address you sign in with.

<RemoveFlow />

GainForest may keep data it has paid for, such as data from funded grants or photos entered into BioBlitz prize rounds.

## Questions

We are still learning how to explain this well. If something here is unclear, or seems wrong, please tell us at [team@gainforest.net](mailto:team@gainforest.net). For sign-in or account deletion, write to [support@hypercerts.org](mailto:support@hypercerts.org).
