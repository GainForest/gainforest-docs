## Meaning of the name

<Cards>
  <Card title="🌏 bumi">Means 'earth' in several Southeast Asian and South Asian languages.</Card>
  <Card title="🏷 certs">Short for impact certificates: records that help represent environmental work and evidence.</Card>
  <Card title="🌱 Bumicerts">A platform for earth-focused impact records, built around Projects and evidence.</Card>
</Cards>

## Who we are building for

Bumicerts is for people and organizations doing environmental work on the ground, including:

* community groups
* Indigenous communities
* local nonprofits
* schools
* project teams
* local organizations in the Global South

Bumicerts is built for groups who are finding ways to:

* prove stewardship
* report impact
* gain visibility
* organize environmental evidence
* attract funding and partnerships

## Public by design

Bumicerts is designed to help communities make their stewardship visible. The essence of the platform is public documentation: Projects, updates, and linked evidence are meant to help others understand what is happening, what work is being done, and why it matters.

This public record can support awareness, trust, impact reporting, funding conversations, and partnerships.

<Callout type="warning">
Because Projects are intended to be public, do not upload or link sensitive information that should not be shared openly. This may include private locations, culturally sensitive knowledge, personal information, or data that could put people, species, or habitats at risk.
</Callout>

## The basic idea

Instead of only describing impact in a report, users can create a **Project** and connect real data uploads as evidence.

<Cards>
  <Card title="1. Create a Project">Set up a page for your environmental work, story, goals, and updates.</Card>
  <Card title="2. Upload data">Add biodiversity observations, photos, field notes, AudioMoth recordings, tree data, drone imagery, files, or external links.</Card>
  <Card title="3. Link evidence">Connect uploads to your Project so others can understand and review the work.</Card>
  <Card title="4. Use the record">Use the Project for impact reporting, visibility, funding conversations, grants, and community updates.</Card>
</Cards>

## What you can do with Bumicerts

Use Bumicerts to:

* create a Project for environmental work
* upload biodiversity observations and other field evidence
* link uploaded data to a Project
* organize evidence in a way that funders, partners, and communities can understand
* explore opportunities such as the Weekly BioBlitz Challenge and the Rewilding the Web Grant
* use tools such as Tainá and AudioMoth bioacoustic support
