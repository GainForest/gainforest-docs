## What the grant supports

The grant helps communities and local organizations document environmental stewardship and turn fieldwork into evidence-backed Projects.

Funding can support:

* capacity building
* AudioMoth bioacoustic sensor deployment, which is required for every selected Project
* basic field equipment
* biodiversity observation workflows
* Project documentation on Bumicerts
* environmental storytelling and impact reporting

## What selected grantees need to do

Selected grantees are required to use **Bumicerts** and related tools on GainForest.app during the program. This is so the grant does not only support equipment, but also creates a public record of stewardship, learning, and evidence.

<Cards>
  <Card title="📁 Bumicerts Project">Create or maintain a Project that explains who you are, what you are doing, where the work happens, and why it matters.</Card>
  <Card title="🎙 AudioMoth bioacoustics">Deploy AudioMoth sensors and link sound recordings or deployment records as Project evidence. Passive audio data collection must be central to your Project.</Card>
  <Card title="🦋 Biodiversity observations">Optionally upload species observations, field notes, photos, and other ecological evidence to support the AudioMoth work.</Card>
  <Card title="🤖 Tainá">Optionally use Tainá as an interactive Telegram field companion for learning, species identification, voice notes, field notes, and biodiversity observations.</Card>
</Cards>

## Bioacoustic sensor support

Every selected Project must include **AudioMoth bioacoustic sensor deployment**. AudioMoth is the only bioacoustic sensor integration currently supported on GainForest.app.

GainForest can provide capacity building around:

* planning a sensor deployment
* setting up devices
* recording deployment information
* uploading sound recordings
* linking recordings to a Project as evidence
* understanding how bioacoustic data can support stewardship and impact reporting

<Callout type="warning">
**Important:** GainForest does **not currently provide post-data processing** for bioacoustic datasets.

This means we can support capacity building, equipment, AudioMoth deployment, uploads, and linking recordings as Project evidence — but we do not currently provide species identification, acoustic analysis, or scientific interpretation after the data is collected.

If your Project needs this kind of analysis, it is best to plan for a Project partner who can support it, such as a local authority, academic institution, researcher, conservation scientist, or biodiversity monitoring expert.
</Callout>
