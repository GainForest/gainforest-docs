## How Bumicerts works

Bumicerts turns environmental work into evidence-backed Projects in five steps.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create a Project
Set up a page for your stewardship, restoration, monitoring, or community work.
</Step>

<Step>
### Upload evidence
Add observations, photos, files, datasets, or AudioMoth recordings.
</Step>

<Step>
### Link evidence to your Project
Connect uploads so others can understand what happened and why it matters.
</Step>

<Step>
### Share updates
Post progress over time to build a public record of your work.
</Step>

<Step>
### Use your Project
Put it to work for reporting, visibility, grants, and funding conversations.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Start with a Project

A **Project** is the main unit on Bumicerts — it brings together the people, place, story, updates, and evidence behind environmental work. If you are unsure where to begin, begin here.
